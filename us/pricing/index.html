What you need

What do you need to watch IPTV?

📶

Internet

At least 16 Mbit/s for Full HD and 25 Mbit/s for 4K per device. An Ethernet cable or strong Wi-Fi gives the steadiest picture.

📺

A compatible device

Smart TV (Samsung, LG), Fire TV Stick, Android TV box, phone, tablet, PC/Mac or MAG box.

📲

An IPTV app

For example IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate, IBO Player or VLC. You add your M3U link or Xtream codes.

💬

WhatsApp

You receive your access and reach our 24/7 support on WhatsApp – we also help with setup.

FAQ

Questions about pricing & payment

How much does 4K IPTV cost?

Plans cost $39.99 (Bronze, 12 months), $49.99 (Gold, 15 + 3 free months), $59.99 (Platinum, 15 + 3 free months) and $84.99 (Exclusive, 24 months) for 1 connection – from about $2.78 a month.

Are there hidden fees or auto-renewal?

No. You pay the price shown once. There is no activation fee, no extra charge and no automatic renewal.

What is a connection?

A connection is one device watching at the same time. To watch on 2 TVs at once you need 2 connections. Each extra connection is 15% cheaper.

How do I pay?

After you order we send a secure payment link on WhatsApp. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer.

When do I get my access?

Usually about 5 minutes after payment, on WhatsApp: an M3U link or Xtream codes.

Can I upgrade or add connections later?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the plan or number of connections you want and we update your access.

Which plan should I choose?

For most people Gold is the best value: 18 months for $49.99. If you want adult channels and the longest period, choose Exclusive with 24 months.

Why are prices in US dollars?

Our prices are set in USD. If you pay in another currency, your bank converts it automatically at the daily rate.

Ready to watch in 4K?

Pick your plan, enter your email and WhatsApp – you will be watching within minutes.
